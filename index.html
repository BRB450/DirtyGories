Guttergories

A dirty twist on Scattergories. 😈

How to Play

  • Click "Start Round" to generate a random letter and category list.
  • Each player must come up with answers that start with the given letter.
  • Each category must have a unique answer — duplicates are not allowed.
  • Answers should be as creative, funny, or dirty as possible (within your group's comfort zone).
  • Players vote on whether answers are acceptable.
  • You can't start a new game while a round is running. Click "Stop Timer" first.
